# 🌐 Web App (PWA) Launch & Installation Guide

The Web version of **"My IMES"** is built with **Progressive Web App (PWA)** technology. It runs on any device directly inside your web browser (iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux) without needing to download `.apk` binaries or sideloading tools.

> 🔗 **Direct Web App Link:**  
> ### [👉 https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/](https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/)

---

## 🍏 Installation on iPhone and iPad (Safari)

By adding the PWA to your iOS Home Screen, you get a native-like experience **without Safari browser bars**, running in standalone full-screen mode!

1. Open [https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/](https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/) **specifically in Safari** (iOS third-party browsers do not support PWA installation).
2. In Safari's bottom toolbar, tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
5. The official "My IMES" icon will appear on your iPhone home screen. Open it just like any native app!

---

## 🤖 Installation on Android (Google Chrome)

1. Open [https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/](https://vladosflexxx.github.io/imes_app/app/) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three dots menu** in the top right.
3. Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Confirm installation. The app will be added to your app drawer and home screen.

---

## 💻 Installation on Desktop (Windows / macOS / Linux)

1. Open the URL in **Google Chrome**, **Microsoft Edge**, or **Brave**.
2. Look for the **"Install My IMES"** icon in the right side of the address bar.
3. Click **"Install"**.
4. The app will launch in a dedicated clean window and pin to your taskbar/dock.

## 🛡️ VPN and Global Access (Smart Gateway)

* **Inside Russia (without VPN):** Connects directly or via our high-speed backup gateway in Yandex Cloud.
* **With VPN active (or outside Russia):** The university LMS limits access from non-Russian IP ranges. Our Web app automatically routes traffic through our smart failover proxy (Cloudflare Workers / HomeLab Proxy). Everything works smoothly worldwide!
